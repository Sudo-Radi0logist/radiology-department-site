Who we are

The diagnostic backbone of the hospital.

The Department of Radiology is the diagnostic backbone of Inlaks & Budhrani Hospital, part of the Sadhu Vaswani Mission's Medical Complex in Pune.

Since the hospital opened in 1989, we have worked on the belief that every patient deserves an accurate answer, delivered with dignity and without financial hardship. Every scan we perform is part of a person's story, and we treat it that way.

Our guiding thought

Every image is part of a person's story.

We approach imaging as an essential part of the patient's care journey—supporting timely decisions while respecting each individual's dignity.

Our direction

Mission, vision and values

The principles that guide our clinical and academic work.

Our mission

To provide accurate, timely and compassionate imaging at an affordable cost, reported by qualified radiologists, performed safely, and always in service of the patient in front of us.

Our vision

To be Pune's most trusted hospital-based imaging department, known for diagnostic excellence, patient safety, and teaching the next generation of radiologists.

Service as worship

We honour the Mission's ideal that serving the patient is a form of devotion.

Accuracy

Every report is reviewed with care, because the clinical decision that follows depends on it.

Safety

We follow the ALARA principle on radiation, use contrast agents carefully, and screen every patient before imaging.

Affordability and access

High-quality imaging should not depend on who can pay.

Collaboration

We work as partners with clinicians, not as a back-office service.

Our place in the complex

Connected, multidisciplinary care

Radiology works hand in hand with Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Nuclear Medicine, Cardiology, Neurosurgery, Orthopaedics, Nephrology and Critical Care.

Joint tumour boards and multidisciplinary meetings help patients get one coherent plan rather than a stack of separate reports.

Teaching and academics

Learning is part of our work.

SVMMC is a DNB-accredited centre with an active academics department. Our department supports the DNB programme, runs case conferences and journal clubs, and takes part in the hospital's CME programmes.
