About the department

Imaging that informs better decisions.

The Department of Radiology supports patient care through diagnostic imaging and works alongside clinical teams across the hospital. This website is designed to bring together information about our services, people, teaching and academic work.

Our priorities

Three connected missions

A contemporary academic radiology department is built on excellent clinical service, thoughtful education and a culture of inquiry.

01

Patient care

Clear information, appropriate imaging and collaboration with referring clinicians to support patient-centred care.

02

Education

Structured learning, case-based discussions and continuing professional development for learners and colleagues.

03

Research

Encouraging scholarly activity, evidence-based practice, publications and responsible innovation in medical imaging.
